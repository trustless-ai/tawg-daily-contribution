# ERC-8183: Agentic Commerce

## Summary

ERC-8183 defines an escrow-backed job lifecycle for agentic commerce. A client funds work, a provider submits a deliverable, and an evaluator completes or rejects the job.

## Status

The canonical ERC is Draft. The normative page and its linked community discussion were fetched and verified at the time in `verified_at`.

## Motivation

Agent-to-agent work needs a minimal settlement kernel that protects funded work, makes evaluation authority explicit, and provides deterministic refund paths.

## Architecture

Each job binds client, provider, evaluator, budget, expiry, status, and optional hook data. Funds remain in escrow until a valid terminal transition pays the provider or refunds the client.

## Interfaces

The core surface covers job creation, provider and budget assignment, funding, submission, completion, rejection, and expired-job refund. Authorization is role- and state-dependent.

## State machine

The main lifecycle is `Open → Funded → Submitted → Completed|Rejected|Expired`. Rejection is also available from Open and Funded under the roles defined by the normative specification; invalid transitions must revert.

## Security considerations

Implementations must protect escrow accounting, authorization checks, expiry handling, hook isolation, reentrancy boundaries, and signer identity when meta-transactions are supported.
