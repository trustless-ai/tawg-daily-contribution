# RVR

RVR is an Ethereum ERC proposal. The proposal PR is at https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1980.

As of August 2026, the proposal had reached formal-draft stage. A group review noted it involves a profile-authority boundary design and cross-referenced ERC-8354 as a worked example of where that boundary is drawn.
